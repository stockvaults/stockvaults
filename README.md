## StockVault

StockVault is a market and liquidity infrastructure layer built for Stock Tokens on Robinhood Chain.

The product brings market discovery, concentrated liquidity, vault strategies, portfolio tracking, and onchain activity into one interface.

### Product

- **Markets** — discover Stock Token markets and live market activity
- **Liquidity** — build and manage concentrated liquidity positions
- **Vaults** — curated strategies and market exposure
- **Portfolio** — track positions and wallet activity

### Technology

`React` · `TypeScript` · `Vite` · `Wagmi` · `Viem` · `RainbowKit`
